Common Problems With Tankless Water Heaters in Denver

Tankless water heaters are designed for long-term performance, but they can still experience operational issues. Recognizing early warning signs may help you determine when it's time to seek professional assistance.

No Hot Water

A complete loss of hot water may result from ignition problems, electrical issues, gas supply concerns, or internal component failures.

Tankless Water Heater Not Heating Properly

If your water never reaches the desired temperature or changes unexpectedly, the system may require diagnostics to identify the cause.

Tankless Water Heater Error Codes

Many modern tankless units display error codes when a problem occurs. These codes can indicate issues involving sensors, airflow, gas supply, ignition, or internal components.

Ignition Failure

When the burner fails to ignite, the system cannot produce hot water. Several factors may contribute to ignition problems, making professional troubleshooting important.

Flame Failure

Some systems automatically shut down if the flame is interrupted. A professional inspection can help determine why this is happening.

Mineral Buildup

Hard water can cause mineral deposits inside the heat exchanger. Over time, buildup may reduce efficiency and affect system performance. Periodic descaling may help reduce these effects.

Cold Water Sandwich Effect

Some homeowners notice a brief burst of cold water between periods of hot water usage. This is commonly referred to as the cold water sandwich and may occur with certain tankless systems.

Water Flow Sensor Problems

Tankless systems depend on flow sensors to detect water usage. If the sensor isn't operating correctly, the unit may fail to activate when hot water is needed.

Heat Exchanger Problems

The heat exchanger is one of the most important components of a tankless water heater. If it becomes damaged or develops excessive scale buildup, system performance may decline.
