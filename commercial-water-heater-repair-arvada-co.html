Overview

Complete Commercial Water Heater Repair Arvada CO Overview

Commercial water heating systems can vary considerably in size, design, fuel source, and application. Proper commercial water heater troubleshooting starts with understanding the equipment and the symptoms being reported.

Commercial Water Heater Not Heating

A commercial water heater not heating can have several possible causes. Depending on the system, the issue may involve a thermostat, heating element, burner, gas valve, electrical component, controls, or another part.

A professional can inspect the system and help identify the likely cause before discussing repair options.

Commercial Water Heater Leaking

A commercial water heater leaking should be evaluated to determine where the water is coming from. Possible sources can include plumbing connections, valves, the pressure relief valve, the storage tank, or another component.

The appropriate solution depends on the source and condition of the equipment.

Gas Commercial Water Heater Problems

Commercial gas systems may use burners, gas valves, ignition components, thermostats, and other equipment to produce hot water.

A problem with these components can affect heating performance. Gas-fired equipment should be evaluated by an appropriately qualified professional.

Electric Commercial Water Heater Problems

Commercial electric water heaters may rely on multiple heating elements and thermostats. A failed heating element or control component can reduce hot water production.

Commercial water heater heating element repair or thermostat-related service may be possible depending on the system.

Pressure and Temperature Problems

Commercial water heating systems can experience pressure or temperature concerns involving the pressure relief valve, expansion tank, controls, plumbing, or other components.

A commercial water heater inspection can help identify potential causes and determine what service may be appropriate.

Commercial Tankless Systems

Commercial tankless water heaters heat water as it is needed. Problems may involve water flow, heat exchangers, sensors, controls, ignition, or other components.

Commercial tankless water heater repair may be available depending on the equipment and provider.

Repair or Replacement?

Not every commercial water heater problem requires replacement. If a component can be repaired and the equipment remains suitable for the property, repair may be an option.

However, an aging or severely damaged system may make commercial water heater replacement worth considering. A professional can evaluate the equipment and explain the available options.
