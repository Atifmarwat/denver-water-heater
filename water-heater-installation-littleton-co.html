Choosing the Right Water Heater

The best water heater depends on your household or property's hot water needs.

Important factors may include:

✓ Household size
✓ Hot water demand
✓ Available installation space
✓ Fuel type
✓ Existing plumbing
✓ Electrical capacity
✓ Gas availability
✓ Budget
✓ Desired system type

A professional can review these factors and explain which options may fit your property.

Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation

Traditional water heaters store heated water inside a tank.

They are commonly available in gas and electric models. A proper installation may involve the water supply, temperature controls, pressure relief valve, expansion tank, drain connections, and other plumbing components.

A professional can inspect the existing setup and explain what may need to be changed for the new unit.

Gas Water Heater Installation

Gas water heaters use a burner to heat the water.

A gas water heater installation may involve the gas line, vent pipe, water connections, temperature controls, and required safety components.

Because gas systems require proper installation and safety procedures, customers should discuss the project directly with an appropriate independent professional.

Electric Water Heater Installation

Electric water heaters generally use heating elements controlled by thermostats.

During an electric water heater installation, the professional may need to consider the existing electrical connection, water supply, plumbing, drain setup, and available space.

Proper setup can help the new system operate as intended.

Tankless Water Heater Installation

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows through the system rather than storing a large volume of hot water.

A tankless installation can have different requirements from a traditional tank system. Depending on the model, the project may involve electrical work, gas supply, venting, plumbing, and changes to the existing water supply.

A professional can determine whether the property is suitable for the selected system.

Understanding Water Heater Installation Cost

The water heater installation cost can vary significantly between properties.

Factors may include:

✓ Type and size of water heater
✓ Equipment selected
✓ Labor
✓ Existing plumbing
✓ Gas or electrical connections
✓ Venting requirements
✓ Removal of an old unit
✓ Additional installation materials
✓ Permit or inspection requirements

For accurate pricing, discuss your specific project with the independent professional before approving installation.
