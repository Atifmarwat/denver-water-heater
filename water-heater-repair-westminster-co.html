Complete Water Heater Repair Westminster CO Overview

What Does Water Heater Repair Involve?

A water heater can experience different problems depending on its type, age, condition, and installation. A professional water heater troubleshooting process can help identify the likely source before deciding whether repair or replacement is appropriate.

Traditional tank systems may include a thermostat, heating element or burner, anode rod, dip tube, pressure relief valve, expansion tank, and water tank.

Tankless systems use different components and may require specialized troubleshooting.

Water Heater Not Heating

A water heater not heating can quickly become inconvenient. An electric system may have a heating element, thermostat, or electrical issue. A gas system may have a pilot light, burner, thermocouple, or gas valve problem.

Professional inspection can help determine what is preventing the system from producing normal hot water.

Water Heater Has No Hot Water

If your water heater has no hot water, the problem may have several possible causes. Sometimes a specific component can be repaired without replacing the entire unit.

An independent professional can evaluate the equipment and explain whether repair, maintenance, or replacement may be appropriate.

Water Heater Leaking

A water heater leaking should be inspected to determine the source. Water may come from a connection, valve, fitting, pressure relief valve, or the tank itself.

The correct solution depends on the location and severity of the leak and the overall condition of the water heater.

Water Heater Making Noise

A water heater making noise may have sediment buildup or another operating issue. Popping, rumbling, or unusual sounds can sometimes indicate that the system needs inspection or maintenance.

A professional can assess the cause rather than assuming that replacement is necessary.

Gas and Electric Water Heater Problems

For gas systems, problems can involve the pilot light, burner, thermocouple, thermostat, gas valve, or venting components.

For electric systems, issues may involve the heating element, thermostat, circuit, or other electrical components.

Because gas appliances and electrical equipment can present safety risks, customers should have these problems evaluated by an appropriately qualified professional instead of attempting unsafe repairs themselves.

Tankless Water Heater Repair

A tankless unit can develop issues involving water flow, heating, ignition, temperature, or internal components. Tankless water heater repair Westminster CO may include professional troubleshooting and maintenance depending on the system.
