Overview

Complete Electric Water Heater Repair Englewood CO Overview

Electric water heaters use electrical heating elements to heat water inside a storage tank. Several components work together to provide hot water, and a problem with one component can affect the entire system.

Electric Water Heater Not Heating

An electric water heater not heating properly can have several possible causes.

The circuit breaker, thermostat, heating elements, wiring, or other components may need to be checked. Instead of replacing the entire system immediately, a professional can evaluate the equipment and determine whether a repair may solve the problem.

Electric Water Heater Has No Hot Water

If your electric water heater has no hot water, the issue may involve a failed heating element, thermostat problem, electrical supply issue, or another component.

Professional troubleshooting can help identify the actual cause and explain your available options.

Electric Water Heater Heating Element Repair

Heating elements are important parts of an electric water heater. When an element fails, the system may produce little or no hot water.

An electric water heater technician can inspect the system and determine whether heating element repair or replacement is appropriate.

Electric Water Heater Thermostat Repair

The thermostat helps control water temperature. A thermostat problem can result in water that is too cool, inconsistent, or unusually hot.

A professional can evaluate the thermostat and related components before recommending a repair.

Electric Water Heater Leaking

An electric water heater leaking should be inspected to determine where the water is coming from.

Possible sources can include connections, valves, fittings, the pressure relief valve, or the water tank itself. If the tank has significant damage, replacement may be more practical than repair.

Electric Water Heater Pressure Problems

Pressure-related issues can involve the pressure relief valve, expansion tank, water supply, or other plumbing components.

Because water heater pressure and electrical components can involve safety concerns, customers should have these issues evaluated by an appropriately qualified professional.
